# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Constellation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior, accept constructive feedback, and prioritize the safety and privacy of users.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/ZShamsi987/Constellation/security/advisories/new) and label the report as a conduct concern. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy to the extent possible and may issue warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans according to severity and pattern.

This compact policy follows the responsibilities and enforcement principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1. A full upstream copy will replace it before the first public release.
